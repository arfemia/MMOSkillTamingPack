This one moves the Taming pack onto Hytale Update 7. It needs an Update 7 server, MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1 and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer, and it won't load on Update 6, so update them together.

### Changes

- **The Taming heading in the achievement book reads in your language.** The heading over the Taming achievements showed the English word Taming whatever language you played in. It reads in your own language.
