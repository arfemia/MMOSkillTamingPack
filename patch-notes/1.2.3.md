Your Taming level rewards now wait for you to collect them, like every other reward.

Built for Update 7: it needs MMO Skill Tree [[1.7.1]] and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer.

### Changes

- The XP boosts you earn at Taming 15, 50 and 100 wait on the Taming skill's Rewards page until you collect them, where they used to arrive on their own. Collecting one puts its boost token on your Boosts tab, ready to start whenever you like.
