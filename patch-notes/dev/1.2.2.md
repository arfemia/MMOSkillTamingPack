The Taming pack's Update 7 release. `manifest.json` declares `ServerVersion` `>=0.7.0-pre.0 <0.8.0` (was `>=0.6.0-pre.13 <0.7.0`) and the dependencies `Ziggfreed:MMOSkillTree ^1.7.1` and `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon >=2.3.0` (were `^1.6.1` and `>=2.1.0`). No shipped id changes.

- **Adds the Taming subcategory's name.** `achievement.category.misc.taming` ships in all nine `Server/Languages/<locale>/mmoskilltree.lang` files (it-IT keeps the word Taming, as the jar's `skill.taming` does), the convention key ZiggfreedCommon's `CategoryNames.achievementSubcategory` reads for the `misc` category's `taming` subcategory that every Taming achievement files under (`Listing.Category` `misc`, `Listing.Subcategory` `taming`). Without it the book fell back to the humanized id, the English word Taming, in every locale.
