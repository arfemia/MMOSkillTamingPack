A picture for every Taming quest, and the repeating ones badged off their own timing. Requires MMO Skill Tree 1.6.1 and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.1.0 or newer; update the mod, the library jar and this pack together.

- **Every quest has a picture.** All six name one, drawn beside the quest wherever it is listed and on the notice when it finishes: a collar for the first bond, a plush for keeping company, the harvest trophy for the beastmaster, seed and meat for the two care dailies, hide for the weekly.
- **The repeating quests stop labelling themselves.** Every quest list badges a quest Daily, Weekly or Repeatable off its own timing, so the hand-written tags are gone and cannot drift from it.
- **Requires MMO Skill Tree 1.6.1 or newer**, which carries a quest's picture onto its notices and draws the badge.
